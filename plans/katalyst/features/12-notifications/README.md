## What it does

An in-app bell telling students about upcoming and overdue activity, XP awards, ready
reviews and challenges — and telling mentors about students who need chasing.

**Notifications are computed on read, not generated by a cron.** Vercel Functions scale to
zero and we have no worker. `GET /api/notifications` runs a handful of queries and returns
what is true right now. The `notifications` table only stores `readAt`, so dismissals
persist.

That is not a compromise for the demo — for this data volume it is genuinely the right
design, and it means there is no scheduler to explain or to fail on stage.

## The rules

**Students**

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| `overdue` | a mandatory course or assessment is past `dueAt` |
| `due_soon` | due within 3 days |
| `review_ready` | a mentor decided on their submission |
| `xp_awarded` | an `xp_event` in the last 24 h they have not seen |
| `challenge` | an active challenge is ≥80% complete, or ends within 24 h |
| `nudge` | streak at risk (checked in yesterday, not today, after 6 PM) |

**Mentors**

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| `escalation` | a submission has waited >24 h for review |
| `escalation` | a student on their course is overdue or inactive 7+ days |

Deterministic ids (`overdue:<assessmentId>`, `xp:<eventId>`) so `readAt` sticks across
recomputations. That is the only subtle part of this feature.
